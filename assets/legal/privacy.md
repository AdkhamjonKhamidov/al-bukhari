# Al Bukhari privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Al Bukhari has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

- **Approximate location.** If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location to work out the Qibla direction and prayer times. The calculation happens on your phone. The last location is saved on your phone, rounded to about one kilometre, so prayer times work offline. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Compass sensor.** Read while the Qibla screen is open. Never stored.
- **Reading position and settings.** Saved on your phone only.
- **Recitations you have played.** Kept on your phone so they replay without internet.

Removing the app deletes all of this.

## What leaves your phone

- **Verse audio.** The first time you play a verse, the app downloads that recitation from the Islamic Network audio service (cdn.islamic.network). As with any internet request, that service receives your IP address and which audio file was requested. We do not receive this information.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
